About us

Built by Mumbai.
For Mumbai.

Bombay Chowkk is a love letter to the city that raised us — a physical space where the old chowk energy meets modern taste. It's food, culture, and community, refusing to be separated.

Our story

Three friends.
One chowk-shaped idea.

Three of us grew up in Lower Parel, Dadar and Bandra respectively. We didn't meet in an office — we met at a Kyani & Co. table in 2019, arguing about which BEST bus route had the best view.

That argument never ended. It just turned into a mood-board, a mood-board turned into a floor-plan, and the floor-plan into Bombay Chowkk.

Our promise is simple: every corner of this chowk must feel like it was made by a Mumbaikar who missed the city, not designed by a brand that studied it.

What we believe

Six principles.

01

Food is not the product.

The evening is. Food is how we hold it together.

02

Nostalgia is a design tool.

Not a costume. We build like Mumbai remembers itself.

03

Community over customers.

You're not billing a table — you're joining a chowk.

04

Local before global.

Home chefs and city artists get the mic before anyone else.

05

Phones down, faces up.

The chowk is a good place to look up from your screen.

06

Hygiene is heritage.

FSSAI-certified, daily audits, published scores. Non-negotiable.
