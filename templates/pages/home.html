About

Not just a food place.
A living Mumbai experience.

Bombay Chowkk is inspired by the old-school Mumbai khau gallis and village chowks where people gathered to eat, laugh, discuss life, celebrate culture, and create memories.

We are building a space where food meets storytelling, community meets entertainment, and nostalgia meets modern experiences.

"This is a place where strangers become friends and every evening feels like a festival."

The signature chowk

A banyan tree,
a chacha, and a chair for you.

At the centre of Bombay Chowkk stands a real banyan tree — wired with a thousand fairy lights. Below it, a circle of mismatched chairs.

This is where storytelling elders, the chacha-characters of Mumbai, sit every evening. They share a paan, an old film story, a Dharavi memory. You sit. You listen. You speak. That's the whole show.

"Yahaan baithoge, toh sheher milega."
