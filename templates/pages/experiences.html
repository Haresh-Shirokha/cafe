Our experiences

Twelve ways to spend
an evening.

Every corner of the chowk holds a different story. Pick your favourite, or make it through all twelve — most regulars do, eventually.

{% for i in "12" %} One destination• Endless cravings• Twenty kitchens• One open sky• {% endfor %}
01

Khau Galli

Twenty-plus rotating Mumbai street food stalls under one chowk. From Bandra bhel to Mohammed Ali kebabs.

02

Live Nataks

Street theatre & Marathi nataks performed beneath the central chowk tree every weekend.

03

Open Mic Nights

Poets, comics, singers — the unfiltered voice of Mumbai gets the stage every Monday.

04

Bollywood Nights

RD Burman to Ranbir. DJ + dhol + dance floor. Friday evenings turn into a chowk-wide baraat.

05

Pani Puri Challenges

How many can you finish before the spice wins? Public leaderboard, weekly champions.

06

Cricket Screenings

Match nights with giant LED, vada pav buckets and the loudest crowd this side of Wankhede.

07

Family Sundays

Magic shows, kids' corner, dhol-tasha and a regional food mela — built for three generations.

08

Food Festivals

Monthly themed pop-ups – Goan, Hyderabadi, Bengali, Gujarati. New cuisine, same chowk.

09

Street Music

Acoustic sets from indie bands, sufi singers and the rare jazz quartet under the lanterns.

10

Community Games

Lagori, carrom, antakshari leagues. Show up alone, leave with a new gang.

11

Poetry Nights

Shayari, slam, kissa-kahani. Hindi, Urdu, Marathi, English — the language of Bombay.

12

Local Artist Showcases

Weekly rotating gallery of Mumbai's home painters, illustrators and craftspeople.
